        Learning to Populate Capacitance Evaluation Tables for
                    Rule-Based Parasitic Extraction
                                                                Anonymous authors




   Abstract—Rule-based parasitic extraction must evaluate geometries
absent from a finite characterization set. We propose Full, an observation-
conditioned residual model that combines a numerical anchor, Transformer
residuals, and applicable numerical corrections through component-
wise gating. Predictions populate eligible native compute-table entries
during preparation, preserving supplied observations, resistance, runtime
lookup, and parasitic-network assembly. Across public rules, finite-
element data, and cross-process transfer, Full lowers condition-level macro
normalized root-mean-square error (NRMSE) relative to the OpenRCX
kernel and multilinear interpolation with sparse-support extension in
all 60 evaluated conditions spanning 10–70% characterization holdout;
Binary and Ungated do so in 55 and 50 conditions. This empirical
consistency motivates Full as the default, without implying optimality
on every condition or downstream metric. Separate fixed-node rule-
export experiments improve capacitance and worst-slack fidelity in all
16 layout–budget settings. A new four-arm sparse-native comparison
completes 48 paired settings with hidden observations removed before
native table construction: Full improves net-capacitance and worst-slack
fidelity over Native-sparse in 7/16 and 9/16 seed-averaged cells. Thus
the offline consistency does not imply universal downstream gains. The
distinct full-characterization compute-table path keeps mean extraction
time within 3.6% of native extraction, with recorded external preparation
stages totaling 5.41–13.99 s.                                                 Fig. 1. Schematic diagram of the rule-driven parasitic parameter extraction

                            VI. C ONCLUSION
   We presented Full, a characterization-conditioned residual model
that uses the available geometry–capacitance samples to improve rule
evaluation. Its numerical anchor and gated correction basis combine
the current process response with learned geometric context. The native
compute-table interface prepares eligible capacitance values before
lookup while preserving measured entries, resistance, and downstream
extraction. Binary and Ungated serve as matched ablations that test
the effects of simplifying or removing gated residual fusion.
   The evaluations connect interpolation accuracy to extraction fidelity.
