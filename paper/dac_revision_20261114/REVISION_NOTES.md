# DAC-oriented research revision

This is a separate editable revision of the locally available main.tex/paper_checked.pdf, reorganized around offline rule construction and native extraction fidelity. Original sources, figures, data, released checkpoints, final_tables and historical runs remain unchanged. The original manuscript's 30%-missing fixed-mask claims are kept separate from new fixed-Q budget measurements.

The revision removes unsupported general extraction, parameter-free target knowledge, unconditional routing necessity and end-to-end speedup implications. It explicitly reports the same-budget adapted-MLP counterexample, retains prior random-outside-support FEM/transfer failures, and treats gate complexity as an empirical choice. The native deployment completes a fixed geometry grid and rewrites only the eligible original downstream holdout tables; it is not arbitrary online inference.

New plots are generated from raw budget and native-run receipts. GP dictionary ridges are documented as 1e-6/1e-4, with no implementation change to match historical artwork. A smaller workflow figure replaces general PEX background artwork. The new paper includes editable Markdown and LaTeX; the local article layout is a research-review format, not a verified current DAC submission template. No submission/publication is performed or recommended merely because compilation succeeds.

Source provenance is recorded in MANIFEST.json and additional_input_verification.json. Related-work adaptation and access limitations are in RELATED_WORK_AUDIT.md and ATTENTION_ADAPTATION.md.
