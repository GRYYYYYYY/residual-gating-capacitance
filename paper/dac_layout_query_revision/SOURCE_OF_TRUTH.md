# Manuscript sources

`main.tex` is the authoritative anonymous DAC-format manuscript (six content
pages, one reference page). `DAC_REWRITE.md` is generated by `sync_markdown.py`.
`extended.tex` / `extended.pdf` retain expanded support-protocol analysis,
the census/error-distance graphics, full runtime discussion, latency/fit table,
and separate native-agreement plot; they are an expanded research treatment,
not a claim of compliance with the six-page submission limit.

All existing data were retained by experimental purpose. The new query census,
native replay, and direct-template experiment have distinct reference identities.
No in-extractor model replacement or new rule-node enrichment is claimed.

The runtime subsection and new tables/figures are generated from completed
measurements by `experiments/runtime_accuracy_20261115/update_paper.py` and
`summarize.py`. The main paper retains the measured census counts and physical
limitations; its census figure remains available in the expanded treatment.
