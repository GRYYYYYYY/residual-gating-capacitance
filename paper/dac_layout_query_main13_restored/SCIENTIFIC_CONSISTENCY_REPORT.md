## Symbols and terminology

S contains observed geometry/value pairs. Q contains query coordinates with withheld values. c gives the process and pattern context. F_theta maps (S,Q,c) to K outputs. The component scales convert physical units to network units for b, Delta and the predicted residual, and back after fusion. Omega is the conjunction of affine and convex support membership. E_i is the valid subset of the 11-slot dictionary E. N_i counts its members. m is a candidate validity flag, pi a component-specific weight. Observed, raw off-grid, effective index, out-of-support, eligible slot and changed getter return each describe a different property.
