This release changes the earlier private snapshot's conservative download-only policy for the four public rule platforms after checking their BSD/Apache terms and preserving notices. The historical input lock's policy fields describe that earlier release; the current redistribution decision is recorded here and in `database/license_sources.json`. No commercial PTF or closed-source foundry corpus is included.

Original PDK rule values are unmodified. `downstream_holdout_v1` rules are explicitly modified copies: only masked capacitance fields are filled by the indicated method; original resistance and unmasked lines are preserved by the exporter. DEF/ODB/SPEF/netlist files are generated by this project from the listed platforms and designs. They are not upstream golden signoff files.

NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch, pandas, Matplotlib, Gmsh, scikit-fem and meshio are installed separately and retain their own licenses. Tool executables, installed environments and upstream Git histories are not bundled. OpenROAD and Yosys revisions are recorded for reproducibility, not relicensed here.

The project has no affiliation or endorsement claim from upstream authors, foundries or tool providers. Original notices in downloaded files must be retained when reusing or redistributing those files.
